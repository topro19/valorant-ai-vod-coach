           VALORANT AI VOD COACH - QUICK START FOR FRIENDS
================================================================================

Welcome! This application analyzes your Valorant gameplay recordings using 
OpenCV combat detection and Google Gemini AI video intelligence with a strict 
PLAYER LOCK system (zero advice derived from teammates or spectator views).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL & SETUP (1-CLICK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simply double-click:
    "Setup.bat"

What this does automatically:
- Checks for Python (and helps install it if you don't have it).
- Creates an isolated environment so it doesn't mess with other programs.
- Installs all required packages (OpenCV, PyWebView, FastAPI, Gemini SDK).
- Creates a "VALORANT AI VOD Coach" shortcut right on your Windows Desktop.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: LAUNCH THE COACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can launch the app at any time by:
- Double-clicking the "VALORANT AI VOD Coach" shortcut on your Desktop
  OR
- Double-clicking "Launch Coach.bat" in this folder.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ENTER YOUR GEMINI API KEY (FREE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the app opens, you will be prompted to connect your Google Gemini API key:

1. Get a 100% free Gemini API key in 30 seconds:
   👉 https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2. Copy your key (starts with "AIzaSy...")
3. Paste it into the setup dialog and click "VERIFY & ACTIVATE KEY".
4. (Optional) If you don't have a key right now, you can click "Offline Demo Mode"
   to test the interface and combat detection immediately!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: ANALYZE YOUR GAMEPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Select your target Agent (e.g., Breach, Jett, Reyna, Cypher).
2. Drag & drop your MP4 gameplay recording, or click "Quick Load Windows Capture"
   to automatically find recordings in your "Videos\Captures" folder.
3. Click "START ANALYSIS".
4. Review the breakdown:
   - Synchronized encounter video clips
   - Root-cause mistake explanations ("Why it was a mistake", "What you should have done")
   - Top 5 positive habits
   - Personalized custom training drills

Have fun climbing the ranks!
